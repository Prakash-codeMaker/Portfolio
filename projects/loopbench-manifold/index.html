Evidence maturity ladder

Level 1 · Computational design / ranking
Level 2 · In-vitro binding
Level 3 · In-vivo distribution / PK
Level 4 · Functional / therapeutic evidence

A binding result does not establish biodistribution or therapeutic function.

Product hypotheses

  • Every readout links to its exact model and design version.
  • Failed, ambiguous and missing records remain visible.
  • Holdout boundaries are enforced before scoring.
  • Metrics connect to a named scientific decision owner.
